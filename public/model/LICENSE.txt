The model files in this folder (poised.bin, ease.bin, turn.bin) are derived from
MakeHuman assets — the base mesh, body-shape targets, default skeleton and skin
weights from https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman — which are released
under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).

They were shaped, posed, subdivided and dressed by scripts/build-model.ts.
